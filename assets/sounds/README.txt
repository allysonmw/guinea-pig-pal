Custom chewing sound
====================

Want your guinea pig to use a real chewing sound?

1. Get a short clip of a guinea pig eating (a few seconds is enough; it loops).
   Use a sound you're allowed to use: one you recorded yourself, or a free/royalty-free
   sound from a site like freesound.org or pixabay.com/sound-effects.
2. Name it  chew.wav  (best: loops with no gap) or chew.mp3 / .m4a / .ogg
3. Put it in this folder (assets/sounds).
4. Restart the app (npm start).

It plays while your guinea pig is eating, and pauses when you pick it up.
Delete the file to go back to the built-in crunching sound.
